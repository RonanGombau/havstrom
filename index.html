Welcome!
My name is Ronan. I am currently sailing with my personnal sailboat, but while doing so, I often pause my trip to work on different boat related projects. Let me know if you need help with anything!

My experience includes:

Deliveries and skippering

On single or monohull sailboats, with crew or solo. In total, I have sailed over 50k nautical miles, of which over 10k as skipper, mostly offshore.

General maintenance

I worked professionally to maintain steel and aluminum boats, doing corrosion repair and light welding jobs, in TIG and MIG. However most of my experience is in fiberglass boats.

Electricity

I have maintained and installed 12V and 220V systems on many boats for years, but my biggest project was the design and full conversion of a sailboat to electric propulsion, with a new 48V system working alongside the 12V, with removal of the old diesel engine and tank, lithium battery pack installation and fitting of the new electric engines.

Mechanics

I have learned my way around engines after years working on different boats and their respective engine rooms. Therefore I can perform all the recurring jobs required of a diesel engine, plus some more important tasks and troubleshooting.

Let’s get in touch if you would like to have a some job done on your boat, so we can determine together whether my skills fit the specific task you have at hand. If you need more than one pair of hands, I can also reach several freelancers that I am very accustomed to work with, and so be able to tackle bigger projects as a team. Like this, things stay simple and affordable for you, and every projects gets the skills, resources and flexibility it needs.
